## Problem

Value Hostelworld Group plc and defend a recommendation in front of a panel, as a four-person team at UCD Smurfit.

## What I did

I was the only team member without a finance background, so I took the product and data lens: the operating numbers underneath the model, and whether they said what we thought they said. I owned two of the four investment theses and the ESG section.

## How it works

The core of my contribution was a revenue bridge. I split the change in net revenue from FY2023 to FY2025 into three drivers: booking volume, bed price and take rate. Volume was positive and bed price was heavily negative. Take rate was the only line that worked. That finding became the first thesis.

## Evidence

- Recommendation: BUY, target price EUR 1.56 against EUR 1.17, upside 33.2%.
- The team reached the live presentation stage of the UCD Smurfit internal round in October 2026.
- Every figure traced to the company's own filings. Share prices were taken from the London line and converted to euro.

## Limits and next steps

The team was not selected to continue beyond the internal round. With more time I would test how sensitive the thesis is to a fall in take rate, since the whole case rests on that one driver.

This was a student exercise and is not investment advice.
